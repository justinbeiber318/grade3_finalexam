ENGLISH GRAMMAR · GRADE 8

Present Simple Tense
Thì hiện tại đơn

Bài luyện tập chia động từ · Câu khẳng định và câu phủ định

I. Câu khẳng định (Affirmative)

Chia động từ trong ngoặc ở thì hiện tại đơn.

1I usually ______ (get) up at 6 o'clock.
2She ______ (go) to school every day.
3They ______ (play) football after school.
4My brother ______ (watch) TV every evening.
5We ______ (have) English lessons on Monday.
6He ______ (do) his homework after dinner.
7The sun ______ (rise) in the east.
8Water ______ (boil) at 100°C.
9The Earth ______ (go) around the Sun.
10Fish ______ (live) in water.

II. Câu phủ định (Negative)

Viết dạng phủ định đúng. Có thể dùng dạng đầy đủ hoặc dạng rút gọn: do not/don't, does not/doesn't.

11I ______ (not / like) coffee.
12She ______ (not / play) badminton every day.
13They ______ (not / go) to school on Sundays.
14He ______ (not / watch) TV in the morning.
15We ______ (not / eat) fast food every day.
16My father ______ (not / work) on Sundays.
17The Sun ______ (not / move) around the Earth.
18Plants ______ (not / grow) without water.
19Fish ______ (not / live) on land.
20The Moon ______ (not / produce) its own light.
